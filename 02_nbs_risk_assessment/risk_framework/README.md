# NbS Risk Assessment Framework

A weighted framework for assessing carbon integrity, environmental risks and procurement suitability of nature-based carbon projects.

## 1. Risk Categories and Weights

The framework uses four risk categories, with weights reflecting their relative importance in the assessment.

| Risk category | Weight |

| Baseline and additionality | 30% |
| Permanence and reversal risk | 25% |
| Leakage and quantification | 20% |
| Safeguards, tenure and governance | 25% |
| Total | 100% |

## 2. Assessment Logic

Each category examines a different aspect of project integrity:

- **Baseline and additionality:** Are the claimed carbon benefits credible and dependent on the project?
- **Permanence:** What could cause carbon losses, and how are reversals managed?
- **Leakage and quantification:** Could emissions be displaced, and are reported carbon benefits supported by evidence?
- **Safeguards and governance:** Are land and carbon rights clear, and are communities and ecosystems protected?

## 3. Procurement Checks

A separate client-requirements check covers registry status, carbon standard, eligible vintage, credit issuance, serial numbers, seller authority and transferability.

**Gate 0 – Exclusion conditions:** Deliberate baseline misrepresentation, material unresolved land-tenure disputes or serious violations of Free, Prior and Informed Consent (FPIC).

A favourable risk assessment cannot override these fundamental concerns or mandatory procurement requirements.

## 4. Decision Approach

The framework follows three stages:

1. **Eligibility:** Check mandatory requirements and exclusion conditions.
2. **Risk assessment:** Evaluate the four weighted categories and document evidence gaps.
3. **Procurement decision:** Proceed, request further verification or hold/exclude the project.

*The weights are proposed analytical judgements developed for this case-study exercise, not official Climeworks policy.*
